# ClauseIQ Sample Bank: Information Governance Policy Handbook

Version 1.0. Approved. This is mock data for the ClauseIQ demonstration.

## 1. Personal-data collection

The bank collects personal data only for a stated and lawful purpose. Business units must record the purpose of each collection in the data inventory before collection starts. Data that is not needed for the stated purpose must not be collected.

## 2. Consent management

Customer consent must be obtained before personal data is processed for any purpose beyond the original service. Consent records are kept in the consent register. A customer can withdraw consent at any time, and withdrawal must be actioned within seven working days.

## 3. Data retention

Records are retained according to the retention schedule. Audit logs and security logs are retained for three years. Customer transaction records are retained for eight years. The Data Governance Lead reviews expired records every month and approves their disposal.

## 4. Access control

Access to systems that hold regulated information is granted on a least-privilege basis and requires approval from the application owner. Privileged access is reviewed every quarter by the IAM Manager. Access is removed within one working day of an employee leaving the bank.

## 5. Audit logging

All systems that process regulated information must generate audit logs of user access, administrative actions and changes to data. Audit logs are protected from alteration. The Security Operations Centre reviews audit logs at least once every quarter and records the outcome of each review.

## 6. Incident response

Security incidents must be reported to the Security Operations Centre immediately on detection. The SOC Manager classifies each incident and leads the response. Incidents that affect regulated information are reported to the regulator as soon as practicable.

## 7. Third-party data sharing

Personal data is shared with a third party only under a written agreement that sets out the purpose, the security measures and the return or deletion of the data. Each third party that receives personal data is risk assessed before data is shared and once a year after that.

## 8. Data deletion

Personal data is deleted when the retention period ends or when a valid deletion request is received. Deletion requests are completed within thirty days. Deletion from backups follows the backup rotation cycle.
